# Two identical bowls on the real SO-101: does an ambiguous prompt give two paths and a specific one a single path?

*pi0.5 SO-100/101 generalist (`hqfang/pi05-so100_101`) as a differentiable flow map on the real arm; 2026-11-07.
Code and data in `robot7/` (this folder); method details and every number in `README.md`.*

## Question

With two identical white bowls in front of the arm, the expectation was that an underspecified prompt
("pick up the bowl", "move the bowl") lets the policy's noise choose between the bowls (two paths), while a
spatially specific prompt ("move to the bowl on the left / right") fixes the choice (one path). This is the
pattern robot5 found for pi0.5 on LIBERO-goal, where underspecified prompts split 6 : 2 by noise seed in closed
loop and trained prompts were deterministic.

## Answer in brief

1. **At the normal noise scale the chunk never chooses.** From the recorded vantage view all 48 seeds of all
   12 prompts end at the bowl under the camera centre (spread 1.1 cm). Scaling the noise opens the other bowl
   for every prompt alike (0 / 1 / 29 / 51 of 96 seeds at scale 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3); left / right wording does
   not move the balance.
2. **The camera view decides.** Across 13 live views, ten degrees of pan (about 4 cm of camera travel) switch
   every prompt from one bowl to the other. Only at the view where the bowls straddle the image centre do the
   ambiguous prompts' plans fan out over both bowls (19 / 48 and 30 / 48 left), and there the longer prompts
   collapse the fan to one tight cluster, but at the same bowl whatever the word says (left, right and center
   all 48 / 48 left at pan 5°, 10 cm). "Specific gives one path" holds; "specific gives the *named* path" does
   not.
3. **The fan is not two modes.** The 48 plan ends form one continuous cloud across the midpoint (largest gap
   under 1 cm against a 2–4 cm spread); the FTLE of the chunk map between a left-bowl seed and a right-bowl
   seed is −1.2 everywhere (contracting), and the plan moves 0.2–0.3 cm per 0.1 noise units along v₁. The
   bowl boundary in noise space is a level set of a smooth contracting map, the "soft label boundary" of
   robot2 and the GMM study, not an LCS ridge.
4. **In closed loop the choice is made by the loop, not by the seed.** 71 episodes from eight start pans:
   "pick up the bowl" goes to the right bowl from every start down to pan −5 (25 of 25) and to the left bowl
   from −6.25 and −7.5 (7 of 8). The right bowl is the loop's attractor; its boundary lies eleven degrees to the
   left of the symmetric view, and the seed decides only within about a degree of it (5 : 1 at −6.25). The
   word "left" shifts that boundary a few degrees to the right ("…on the left" reaches the left bowl from −5
   and sometimes from +1.25 / +2.5); "…on the right" never pulls right. The prompts that are seed-dependent in
   closed loop are the specific ones, the opposite of the expectation.
5. What does produce two distinct paths from one observation on this hardware remains the policy's own
   language axis (the prompt-embedding direction v₁ of 2026-11-06, both bowls reached on command) or a noise
   scale of 2 or more; what produces one path is any prompt from any view that is not on the loop's boundary.

## Setup

- **Policy and flow map.** pi0.5 base fine-tuned on 1,209 SO-100/101 datasets; 50 × 6 absolute joint targets
  per chunk, 1600-D noise, 10 Euler steps; run as an explicit flow map (`so101flow.py`, float32 on the Jetson
  Thor) with the chunk pushed through the arm's forward kinematics so that a plan is the 50-step tcp path in cm.
- **Scene.** Two identical white bowls 9–10.5 cm apart at (24.5, +0.6) and (22.5, −9.8) cm in the base frame
  (located by unprojecting their pixel centres onto the rim plane); wrist camera only; lights on.
- **Open-loop measurement** (`modes7.py screen`). For one observation (frame + measured joints) and one prompt,
  N seeds z ~ N(0, T²I) → N plans; each plan labelled by the bowl nearest its end; the choice coordinate q is the
  end projected on the bowl-to-bowl axis (0 = midpoint, + = left). Reported: left / right counts, mean and
  spread of q, the largest gap in sorted q (bimodality), and optionally the prompt-space ridge of `so101flow`.
- **Observations.** Last night's vantage frame and 13 recorded descent states; 13 live views captured this
  morning by moving the arm through a pan sweep at two heights (`modes7.py views`).
- **Closed loop** (`live7.py`). From a start pose, replan every 40 actions, 25 s, every episode its own seed,
  a 6 cm tool floor so that no bowl is touched; outcome = the bowl nearest the final tcp (`report7.py`).

## What was tried

| knob | range | effect on "two paths vs one" |
|---|---|---|
| prompt wording | 6 ambiguous, 6 left / right, 7 other anchors, 2 absent objects (19–25 prompts) | none at scale 1 from the vantage view; "…in the center" is the only phrase that moves the balance (27 % left at T 1.5) |
| noise scale T | 1, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 3 | the second bowl appears at T ≥ 2 for every prompt alike |
| state along a descent | 13 recorded replan states | unimodal at 11; one broad cloud across the midpoint at the two states with the jaws 4–5 cm up between the bowls |
| camera view (live) | pan −5 … 25°, tool 18 and 10 cm | the decisive variable: the bowl under the camera wins; symmetric view ⇒ fan of plans for ambiguous prompts, single cluster for longer prompts |
| closed-loop start pan | +5, +2.5, +1.25, 0, −2.5, −5, −6.25, −7.5 | one bowl per start except within a degree of the loop's boundary (−6.25: 5 : 1) |
| noise-space LCS | segment between a left and a right seed, FTLE, margins along v₁ | no ridge: FTLE −1.2, smooth label change |

## Interpretation

The generalist's chunk is a smooth, contracting function of the observation: its mean is set by where the
camera looks, the noise adds a jitter of 1–2 cm, and the language changes the plan only through the policy's
own embedding axis, not through the dictionary meaning of "left" and "right". A bifurcation in noise space would
need two separated basins; the data show one basin whose centre slides with the view and whose width grows with
the noise scale. The closed loop then turns a few centimetres of first-chunk bias into a committed bowl, which is
why the same start pose gives the same bowl with every seed and why the seed matters only on the loop's
boundary, exactly where robot5 found LIBERO's splits. For a stop-and-ask monitor this means the question "which
bowl?" cannot be read from the noise of this policy; it can be read from the prompt-space ridge (yesterday's
demo) or from the loop's sensitivity to the view.

## What would make the expected pattern appear

- A policy that grounds spatial words. This checkpoint reads "left / right / center" weakly ("left" shifts the
  closed-loop boundary by a few degrees, "right" not at all); colour words are grounded far better in its
  training mixture, so two bowls of different colours with "pick up the blue bowl" against "pick up the bowl" is
  the cheapest version of the intended experiment.
- Starting the loop on its boundary (pan ≈ −6° for these bowl positions) gives the LIBERO-style seed split for
  the ambiguous prompt; a specific prompt from the same start still goes to the bowl the word does not name.
- Noise scale 2 or more gives two paths from any view, for every prompt.

## Reproduce

```
# open loop (Thor, ~7 s per 48-seed cell, the prompt ridge adds ~65 s per prompt)
VLM_DTYPE=float32 ./thor_py.sh modes7.py views --poses "15 -10 -25 95 -69 30" "5 -10 -25 95 -69 30" ...
VLM_DTYPE=float32 ./thor_py.sh modes7.py screen --obs <views dir> --bowls u v u v --seeds 48 --temps 1 1.5
python modes7.py fig out/modes_<ts>.json; python modes7.py fan out/modes_<ts>.json --cells "views:4|pick up the bowl" ...
# closed loop by seed (8 min startup on the Thor, 35 s per episode)
VLM_DTYPE=float32 ./thor_py.sh live7.py run --monitor off --z-min 0.06 --duration 25 --max-start-jump 20 \
    --episode "pick up the bowl@-6.25,-10,-25,95,-69,30" ...
python report7.py runs/<ts> --bowls-cm 24.5 0.6 22.5 -9.8 --fig figs/x.png; python seedvideo7.py runs/<ts>
```
